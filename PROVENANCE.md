## Country content: consent and credit

The game's Country content is reviewed by Darug, Gundungurra and Wiradjuri organisations before any public release (SPEC.md, "History and Country content"). This section is the record of that review: what each organisation has approved, who approved it and when, how it is credited, and its right to withdraw. An entry is written only after the organisation has checked its wording.

### How the review is run

It follows two published standards, and an organisation may hold the project to either:

- **Terri Janke's True Tracks principles** for Indigenous Cultural and Intellectual Property (ICIP): respect; self-determination; consent and consultation; interpretation; integrity; secrecy and privacy; attribution; benefit sharing; maintaining Indigenous cultures; recognition and protection.
- **The AIATSIS Code of Ethics for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Research (2020)**: Indigenous self-determination; Indigenous leadership; impact and value; sustainability and accountability.

The terms, as sent to every organisation in its review pack:

1. **Paid.** The review is paid at the organisation's own fee, whatever it decides about the text.
2. **Line by line.** The organisation approves, changes or cuts each line by its number in the pack (Darug D1–D12, Gundungurra G1–G15, Wiradjuri W1–W8). A line nobody has approved is not described as reviewed.
3. **Their words stay theirs.** Text an organisation writes, and anything it tells the project about Country, remains its ICIP. It is used only in Ridge Road and its teacher guide, only as approved, and nowhere else without asking again.
4. **Credit in their words.** The game names an organisation, or says it reviewed the game, only in wording the organisation has agreed.
5. **Withdrawal.** An organisation may withdraw approval of any line, or its credit, at any time. The next build removes it, and this record keeps the date.
6. **Nothing added on their behalf.** No Aboriginal language words, place names, stories, pictures of people or huts, or text for the reserved card go into the game unless an organisation writes or approves them.
7. **Last look.** The final text goes back to each organisation before release.

Until an organisation has answered, the game stays with its testers and goes to no school.
